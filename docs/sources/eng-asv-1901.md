# American Standard Version (1901) source record

## Selection rationale

The American Standard Version (1901) is the second English corpus selected for Bible OS because it provides a controlled historical contrast to the World English Bible Protestant Edition already registered by the project.

The World English Bible is a modern public-domain update of the ASV. Pairing the two creates an unusually useful first comparative axis: closely related translation lineage with roughly a century of linguistic modernization between them. This supports future measurement of lexical modernization, grammatical drift, divine-name treatment, punctuation change, and versification stability while limiting confounding variables from language and broad canon differences.

- American Standard Version (1901)

- language English

The copyright statement explicitly says the translation is in the Public Domain and may be copied freely.
