# American Standard Version (1901) source record

## Selection rationale

The American Standard Version (1901) is the second English corpus selected for Bible OS because it provides a controlled historical contrast to the World English Bible Protestant Edition already registered by the project.

The World English Bible is a modern public-domain update of the ASV. Pairing the two creates an unusually useful first comparative axis: closely related translation lineage with roughly a century of linguistic modernization between them. This supports future measurement of lexical modernization, grammatical drift, divine-name treatment, punctuation change, and versification stability while limiting confounding variables from language and broad canon differences.

## Official source evidence

- Official details page: https://ebible.org/find/show.php?id=eng-asv
- Official copyright statement: https://ebible.org/eng-asv/copyright.htm
- Official USFM archive: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-asv_usfm.zip
- Official artifact directory: https://ebible.org/scriptures/dir.php

The official eBible.org pages identify the work as:

- American Standard Version (1901)
- abbreviation ASV
- language English
- dialect Archaic American
- public domain

The copyright statement explicitly says the translation is in the Public Domain and may be copied freely.

## Registration boundary

This record authorizes source evaluation and ephemeral verification only. It does not assert that:

- the archive has already been cryptographically pinned;
- the archive structure has been accepted by an importer;
- every verse locator matches WEBP or another versification system;
- any ASV text has been retained in Git;
- any mapping has been reviewed;
- any corpus or dataset release is publication eligible.

The registered acquisition target remains `registered` until a controlled CI acquisition confirms the exact byte count, SHA-256 digest, response metadata, and safe ZIP structure.
